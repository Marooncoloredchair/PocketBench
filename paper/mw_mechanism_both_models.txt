------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER: OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS. Pocket2Mol makes molecules LIGHTER and
DiffSBDD makes them HEAVIER under identical pocket contraction, both
significant. This is consistent with genuinely distinct mechanistic
responses rather than one shared mass shift scored two ways -- BUT it
is confounded with pocket set (disjoint panels), so it cannot yet be
attributed to architecture. The paper must state that confound.
